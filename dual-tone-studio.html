Dual-Tone Studio

Put a black-and-white picture on a 3D model. Everything runs in this page: nothing is uploaded and no Python is needed.

Already have a 3MF with the texture on it? Choose only the 3MF and press Open: the editor opens it as it is. Block-colour pictures (logos, silhouettes, line art) are turned black and white automatically. Photos and shaded images open the halftone editor first: pick a look, then press Apply to 3D model. The 3D editor then covers the whole part with the picture; clear it, paint or flood-select the triangles you want, move the projector, swap or add pictures, and download the finished 3MF.
